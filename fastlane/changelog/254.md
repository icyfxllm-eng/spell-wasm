The menu's close button moved. It was a small cross in the top-right corner, which on a tall phone sits right next to the battery icon — about the hardest spot on the screen to reach one-handed, and easy to mis-tap. It is now a large button in the bottom-right corner of the menu, where your thumb already is, directly below where the menu button opened it.

It is pinned there, so it does not scroll away: wherever you are in the list, the way out is in the same place. Tapping the dimmed area to the left still closes the menu too, and so does the Escape key if you use a keyboard.

Also fixed: menu rows were scrolling up underneath the clock and battery icons at the top of the screen. The menu had no allowance for that area at all, so the first row could sit behind the status bar. It does now.

Smaller things, in the same area: the last row in the menu (Credits) can now be scrolled fully clear of the close button, and if you use VoiceOver, focus returns to the menu button after the menu closes instead of dropping you at the top of the screen.

What is most useful now:

• Can you close the menu comfortably one-handed, with the hand you normally hold the phone in?
• Scroll the menu all the way down — is the close button ever in the way of the last row?
• Is anything still sliding under the clock at the top?
• If you are left-handed, is the bottom-right corner awkward? It was put there for right-thumb reach and that is worth checking against real hands.
