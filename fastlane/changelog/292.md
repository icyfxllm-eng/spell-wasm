One keyboard for every game, and tidier Impossible word lists.

• Every game now uses the same Spell keyboard, so keys look and feel the same everywhere (Spelluzzle keeps its 40-point keys).
• In BoardGame, German Impossible words get a Shift key, so German words can be traps now.
• New setting: "Use device keyboard". Turn it on to type answers with your phone's own keyboard instead of Spell's. Check stays where it is. Pasting or dictating a whole word is still not allowed.
• Impossible word lists: words that are really the same word spelled another way were taken out in Spanish, Russian, German and Korean. A few English, Spanish, Russian and German second spellings are now accepted as right.
• Everything from build 290 is still here.
• Please play a few rounds in different modes, try "Use device keyboard", and tell me if any key looks wrong or a word you typed right was marked wrong.
