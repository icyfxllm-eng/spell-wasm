Good news first: the microphone is fixed.

The last build changed how the app asks the phone for the microphone, and it worked. Where the incoming sound used to measure about 0.0005 — effectively nothing — it now measures 0.29 when someone speaks. That is a real voice, clearly captured, roughly five hundred times stronger than before. The app registers that you are speaking, and hands every scrap of it to the part that turns sound into letters.

That part is now returning nothing, and this build is about finding out why.

Here is the awkward detail. When the speech recogniser failed, the app threw away its explanation and reported "no speech heard" instead. So for six builds the app has been insisting nobody said anything, while quietly binning the one message that would have said what was actually wrong. That message is now kept and shown.

There are a few likely answers, and two of them are settings on your phone rather than anything I can fix in code — the on-device speech model not being downloaded, or dictation being switched off. The next screenshot will say which.

What is most useful now:

• Tap the orb, tap the mic, say the word's letters, and photograph the small readout. If it contains "err=" followed by a name and a number, that is exactly what I need.
• If letters DO land this time, say so and ignore everything above — the previous fix may have been enough on its own.
• As always, typing, word audio, Replay and Slow should be untouched.

Thank you for the screenshots. Every one of them has ruled something out, and this problem has gone from "the microphone hears nothing" to "the microphone hears perfectly and one component is refusing to say why it is unhappy". That is a much smaller problem.
