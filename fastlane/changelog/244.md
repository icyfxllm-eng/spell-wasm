The Spelling Bee sounds different in this build, and that is the point.

Until now the Bee used your phone's own built-in voice, while every other mode played a recording from our server. So the Bee was the one place none of the recent audio work reached — and it is the mode where hearing the word is the whole task. It now plays the same audio as everywhere else: the word has a little silence at each end so its first and last sounds are not clipped, and it sounds the same on every phone instead of depending on your iOS version. "Slower" is now a separately recorded slow reading rather than the same recording played at a slower speed.

If the Bee sounds better, or worse, or just different to you, that is the single most useful thing to report from this build.

Also:

• "Can't hear it" is now in Spell Picture too, on every subject — it used to give you a slow reading only on the easier ones.
• Spell Cross squares stop shrinking at a minimum size. On the hardest levels the board now scrolls a little instead of squeezing the squares down to something you cannot tap. Tell us if you would rather it scrolled less and shrank more.

From the last build, still worth a look if you have not tried it: "Can't hear it" in SpellDoku, Spell Search and Spell Cross, and both of those two fitting on a phone screen without running off the bottom.

If a word is hard to make out after hearing it slowly, tell us the word and the language. Two have already been confirmed that way.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
