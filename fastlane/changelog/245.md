Nothing in this build changes what you see or hear. It is a housekeeping build, and I would rather tell you that than invent something for you to try.

The work behind it is on our server, not in the app. Two testers reported words that are hard to make out, and we put two speech recognizers on sixty English recordings to check. They heard "half" as "have" and "leaf" as "leave", which matches what you told us — so the reports were right, and the cause is the reading itself rather than the recording being cut short. This build adds the machinery to fix a specific word's pronunciation one word at a time, and to compare voices before changing one. Neither is switched on yet. You will hear the difference in a later build, not this one.

Still the most useful things to try, from the last two builds:

• The Spelling Bee's audio, which changed completely in build 244.
• "Can't hear it", now in SpellDoku, Spell Search, Spell Cross and Spell Picture.
• Spell Cross and SpellDoku on a phone screen — they should fit without running off the bottom.

If a word is hard to make out after hearing it slowly, tell us the word and the language. Three have been confirmed that way so far, and that is exactly how the two above were found.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
