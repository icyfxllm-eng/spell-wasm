If you play in Arabic, this build fixes something that was marking you wrong.

Arabic is normally written without the short-vowel marks, and that is how the game stores its words. But writing the marks is correct Arabic, and the iPhone's Arabic keyboard puts them one long-press away. If you typed them, the game called the word misspelled. It no longer does — مدرسة and مَدْرَسَة are the same spelling and both are accepted, in Kid Mode too.

What has not changed, deliberately: the letters still count. ة and ه are still different, ى and ي are still different, and the hamza seats أ إ ا ؤ ئ are still graded. Those are spelling. Only the vowel marks, the decorative stretch character, and the invisible joiner are ignored now.

If you play in Hindi, nothing changes for you in this build — but the same check now covers Devanagari, so a typed half-form joiner will not cost you a word either.

Everyone else: nothing you can see. The rest of this build is a new check on the Arabic and Hindi word lists that runs every time we build, so a bad edit to a script none of us can read gets caught by the machine instead of by you.

Still the most useful things to try, from the last few builds:

• The Spelling Bee's audio, which changed completely in build 244.
• "Can't hear it", now in SpellDoku, Spell Search, Spell Cross and Spell Picture.
• Spell Cross and SpellDoku on a phone screen — they should fit without running off the bottom.

If a word is hard to make out after hearing it slowly, tell us the word and the language. That is how the two confirmed audio problems were found.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
