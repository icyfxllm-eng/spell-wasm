SpellDoku is what to try in this build. It has changed a lot since the last one you had.

A 9x9 board now fits on a phone. The board, the symbol key, the keyboard and the buttons are all on screen at once — no scrolling. Backspace and the check mark sit at the end of the bottom letter row now. If you ever have to scroll to reach a key, that is a bug: tell us which phone and which board.

The chip at the top says Letters or Numbers, whichever the board in front of you is using, and it keeps up as you flip through boards. It used to say "Numbers" over a board full of letters.

Tier Mode is new. Each digit stands for a difficulty band, and you spell a word from that band instead of the number word. The picker at the top switches it on.

The symbol key has definition cards — tap the "?" next to a symbol to see what its word means. It should never give the spelling away. If one ever does, that is worth reporting.

12x12 boards are gone. They did not work on a phone screen.

Spell Cross builds tighter grids with fewer stray gaps.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
