A small build, and an honest one: it removes something that never worked.

If you play in Korean, the microphone for spelling out loud is gone. It was offered, and it could not succeed. Korean is written in syllable blocks, and the letters the app produced when you spoke were a different kind of letter from the ones a Korean word is built out of — so a player who said every letter of a word correctly was still marked wrong, every time. Rather than leave a button that is guaranteed to fail, it is no longer offered for Korean.

Making it genuinely work needs the app to assemble spoken letters into syllable blocks the way a Korean keyboard does. That is a real piece of work and it is not in this build. The Korean letter names the app already knows are correct and are kept, ready for that day.

Nothing changes for any other language. If you spell out loud in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Polish, Vietnamese, Japanese, Filipino, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Swahili or Hindi, the mic behaves exactly as it did in the last build.

What is most useful now:

• Korean players: confirm the mic is simply absent, and that typing is unaffected. If you still see a mic anywhere in Korean, that is the bug to report.
• Everyone else: if the mic changed in any way in your language, that is also a bug — nothing was meant to move for you.
• If you spell out loud regularly in a language other than English or Spanish, I would like to know how often it mishears a letter. Those letter lists were drafted by machine and have not yet been checked by a speaker, so your experience is the only evidence that exists.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
