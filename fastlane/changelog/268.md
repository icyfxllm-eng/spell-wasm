Voice spelling works. Say the letters and they land in the box.

It has taken an embarrassing number of builds to get here, and it turned out to be five separate faults stacked on top of each other — each one hiding the next, each one capable on its own of producing exactly the same symptom of nothing happening.

The phone's audio system was handing the microphone to whatever played a word, so the mic went deaf mid-sentence. The recording mode in use switched off the automatic gain, so what did arrive was about a hundred times too quiet. The app then compared that against a loudness threshold set five times higher than the signal, so it concluded nobody had spoken. When that was fixed, the app was interrupting the speech recogniser after every letter, long before it had enough to work with. And finally, with all of that repaired and the recogniser correctly returning "C", "A", "T", the function that writes letters into the box called itself in a loop and crashed before writing anything.

That last one is why every earlier fix appeared to change nothing. Four real repairs were invisible because the final step could never have worked.

Two things also removed in this build that were making it feel slow: diagnostic logging that ran on the audio path itself, and a status readout updating five times a second. Both were scaffolding from the hunt, and neither belonged in a build you use.

What is most useful now:

• Spell words out loud and tell me how it feels. Accuracy, speed, letters that come out wrong.
• There is a delay of roughly a second between saying a letter and seeing it. That is the phone's speech recognition thinking, not the app waiting, and there are a couple of levers left to try.
• It is English and Spanish that have been checked by a person. The other twelve languages use letter names drafted by machine and not yet reviewed by a speaker, so if you spell out loud in those, errors are expected and worth reporting.
• Typing is unchanged, as it has been throughout.

Thank you for the screenshots and the patience. Plugging the phone in at the end is what cracked it: reading what the app actually did, live, found in one hour what ten rounds of guessing could not.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
