The menu is one menu now. Tap the three-line menu button in the top-right corner and everything is there: every way to play, your own words, your misses, your account, settings. The three round icons, the gamepad sheet behind one of them and the row of chips under the wordmark are all gone, and the home screen is 54 pixels shorter for it — the orb sits higher and closer to your thumb.

Two things that used to crash the app are fixed:

• Leaving a Spell Picture round froze everything. Any exit from a picture — the X or Continue after the reveal — took the whole app down, and the only way back was to close and reopen it. That has been broken since the end of July.
• Chinese and Japanese handwriting practice crashed the moment the pad opened, so it was effectively unusable.

Both were the same kind of mistake: code reaching for a piece of the screen that had been renamed or removed. There is now a check that fails the build if any of that code points at something that is not there, so this particular way of breaking cannot come back quietly.

Also fixed, all found while building the new menu: The Climb from the menu now starts a Climb run instead of opening the leaderboard. Saving a word list no longer empties the menu. And Spell Jr is no longer offered a sign-in.

What is most useful now:

• Can you find everything you used to reach? If something you used is missing from the menu, that is the report I want most.
• Does the menu button feel like the obvious place to look, or did you hunt for it?
• Spell Picture: play a few rounds and leave them in different ways — the X, Continue, starting over.
• If you write Chinese or Japanese, the handwriting pad should now open.

One thing it deliberately will not do yet: the menu will not open while a timed round is running, so it cannot cost you the clock. Tell me if that gets in your way.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
