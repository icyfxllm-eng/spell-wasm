If your app is set to a language other than English, the menu is finally in that language. The three big headings over the list of games — Spelling, Word puzzles, Meaning — were English on every phone, in all fifteen languages, because the words were stored as the English text rather than translated. Everything else in the menu was already localized, so those three headings were the only English left, and they are the biggest text on the screen.

The Climb is translated too. It was already in your language in the pill under the wordmark, but the menu row still said "The Climb" in Spanish and Chinese, and the two did not always match. They use the same word now, so what the pill calls it is what the menu calls it.

Spell Jr is in your language as well.

These translations have not been checked by a native speaker yet. That is deliberate rather than sloppy: a reasonable word in your own language beats English while we wait for the review round, and every one of them is queued for a speaker to correct. If any of them reads oddly, or is simply wrong, tell us the language and what it should say — that is the most useful thing you can send about this build.

One English change: the first heading reads "Spelling" rather than "Spell it", because "Spell it" was also the name of a game in the list directly beneath it.

There is now a build check that fails if a tracked piece of text is left as English in a translated language. The old check only asked whether the text existed, which is exactly why this slipped through.

What is most useful now:

• Set your app to your own language and open the menu. Does every heading read naturally?
• Does The Climb read the same in the menu as it does in the pill under the wordmark?
• Spell Jr in your language — does the name work, or is it awkward?

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
