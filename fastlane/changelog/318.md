This build tidies the English app for launch. Everything else is the same as build 317.

What changed:
• Spelldoku, Spell Search, Spell Cross and Board Game now come with SpellGame English. Without it they are not in the menu. Little Speller still has them.
• Letter Forge is gone for now.
• Word Chain, Impostor, Spelling Bee and the Letter Forge screen no longer sit under the clock, so Exit can always be tapped.
• Impostor no longer makes rude words out of misspellings.
• Settings is shorter: the Spell It, photo list, word stories and online switches, the two debug rows, the volume sliders and "Play online with family" are gone, and so are "How this works" and Credits in the menu.

What is most useful now:
• Without SpellGame English, check the menu: are Spelldoku, Spell Search, Spell Cross and Board Game gone? Buy or restore English: do they come back?
• Play Word Chain and Impostor: can you reach Exit at the top at every point, the end screen included?
• Play a few rounds of Impostor. Any card that is a real or rude word, tell us the word.
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
