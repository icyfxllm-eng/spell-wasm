Guardian Report: a weekly report for grown-ups.

• Your Words → Guardian Report (behind the parent check, with SpellGame Complete): last week's words by type, Mastered / Working on / New, and a practice list of the words your child missed.
• Add to My Words saves that list as "Week of …", once per week.
• Share sends the report as plain text.
• Optional Monday reminder at 08:00, off until you turn it on: a count only, never a word. It replaces the dashboard's weekly digest; both toggles are the same setting.
