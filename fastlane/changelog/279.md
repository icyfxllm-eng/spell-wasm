Spelluzzle grows up: seven additions, all switched on for this device pass.

Pencil runes: press and hold a cell to leave a note on a rune. Notes are yours only and never count as an answer.
Rune strip: a row of every rune on the board, so you can see which ones you have cracked.
Ripple: when a word lands, the letters spread to the other words with a soft tick.
Par boards: a board type with a listen budget. Solve it within par and you are under par; a word you never listened to counts in your favour.
Expeditions: two boards that share one cipher, then a vault. The runes you learned carry over.
My Words boards: a board built from a word list of your own. It needs at least three matching words and never changes the list.
Rune Duel: two players pass the phone, or you play the computer. Claim words, pass, and see each verdict.

What is most useful now:

• Pencil: is a long-press discoverable, or did you never find it?
• Par: does the budget feel fair, or does it make Listen feel expensive?
• Expedition: does the carried cipher feel like a reward on board 2?
• Duel: is it clear whose turn it is and who owns each claimed word? Does the computer feel beatable?
• Small phone: any row, key or header that is clipped or too tight?
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
