A new puzzle mode: Spelluzzle, in English.

Every other mode in Spell grades one word at a time. In Spelluzzle the words on a board depend on each other, so a word you are sure of helps you crack one you are not.

A board is five to seven words written in runes. One rune is one letter, and the same rune is the same letter everywhere on the board. Tap a word, listen, and spell it. The letters you typed then appear, dimmed, in every other word that uses the same runes. On the harder levels some words are never spoken, and you work them out from the letters you already have. A secret word is never spoken until the board is solved.

When two of your words disagree about a rune, that rune turns amber everywhere. Nothing tells you which word is wrong. Working that out is the puzzle. There is no timer, no lives and no red: you earn up to three stars for a board (Solved, Sharp ear, Codebreaker), and New board is always free.

You will find it in the menu under Word puzzles. English only for now. Spell Jr gets smaller boards of Easy and Medium words.

What is most useful now:

• Does spelling the first word feel like a reward, with letters appearing in the other words?
• Spell a word wrong on purpose. Does the amber on the shared rune make sense, and is it clear that the app is not telling you which word is the wrong one?
• Do any of the rune shapes look like a letter or a symbol you already know? That is the thing I most want to hear about.
• On Medium and above, can you work out a silent word without using Listen? Does Listen ever give a letter away?
• Play ten boards in a row at one level. Do they feel different from each other?
• Hard and Expert on a small phone: are the longer words easy to read and tap?
• Leave in the middle of a board and come back. Is it waiting for you?

New here? Tap the glowing orb in Spell to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
