A new Persian (فارسی) word list: every word is a real dictionary word.

• All 8,340 Persian words are now dictionary headwords (Wiktionary), chosen by how common they are in a million-sentence Persian corpus. Names (آلاباما, آرتور), word fragments and inflected forms (آمده‌اند, کشورشان) are gone.
• Levels follow Persian's own difficulty: common words with no tricky letters are Easy; the س/ص/ث, ز/ذ/ض/ظ, ت/ط, ه/ح and غ/ق traps grow with each level, and rare words sit in Expert.
• Words like استفاده, صورت, ثبت and قدرت, missing before, are in.
• Everything from build 300 is still here.
