Spelling out loud now actually puts the letters in the box. If you tried it before tonight and gave up, this is the build to try again.

Three separate faults were stacked on top of each other, and each one looked the same from the outside — letters that never arrived, or arrived twice.

The first was the microphone deciding how loud "speech" is during the first third of a second after you tap the mic. That is about the length of one spoken letter, so if you started straight away it measured your own voice and set the bar there. Everything you said afterwards fell under the bar. The only way through was to shout, which is exactly what testing it felt like.

The second was a safety timer firing every second and a half and being mistaken for a real detection. It was adding whatever letter was in flight each time it went off. Say "book" and you could get "boookk".

The third is the one that hid the longest. The phone was listening to the whole session as a single stream of speech rather than as separate letters, so it tried to make words out of it. Spelling "book", it heard the final K and wrote down "OK" — the ordinary English word — and since that is not a letter name, your K was thrown away and the word came out "boo". Each letter now gets its own recognition, so there is nothing for it to run together.

Also fixed: Check Spelling refused to work after you spelled a word with your voice. The app has a guard that rejects text dropped in by the phone's dictation, and voice-spelled letters were being caught by it. Your answer would sit there, correct, and the button would do nothing.

What is most useful now:

• Spell a word at whatever speed feels natural. Do not slow down for it — if letters run together when you go at your normal pace, that is the thing I most need to know.
• Words with a double letter, like book or little, are the hard case. Do both letters land?
• Check Spelling should work after voice spelling. If it ever refuses, say which word.

Still wrong, and known: B, C, D, E, G, P, T, V and Z sound nearly identical to a phone, so B still comes back as P sometimes. Nothing in this build addresses that — the fix for it is being built next. In the meantime "B as in boy" and the Bravo/Charlie/Delta alphabet both work and both sidestep it entirely.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
