There is a new button on the play screen: "Can't hear it".

If a word is hard to make out, tap it. You can hear the word again slowly, or ask to be shown it. Asking to be shown it costs you nothing — that round simply does not count. No score, no shield spent, your streak stays where it was, and the word does not go into your misses. A replacement word takes its place so a run is not cut short. In a Climb run you can do this once, in the Daily twice, and in practice as often as you like.

The word is only shown to you. It is never typed into the answer box for you, and you cannot submit it.

That is there because "half" was reported as impossible to hear, and a player who cannot hear a word is being tested on guessing, not spelling. Everything above works with no connection and sends nothing.

Underneath it, every word now has a little silence added before and after it, so a word's first and last sounds cannot be clipped off by your phone as playback starts. Swahili audio is at a higher bitrate. Every clip is being regenerated, so the first play of a word may take a moment longer than usual.

Also: a Korean word that no keyboard could actually type has been removed from the word bank.

If a word is still hard to hear after all this, that is worth telling us about — say which word and which language.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
