Photographing a word list should need no tidying up afterwards. This build is the cleanup: list numbers, letters and bullets come off, trailing full stops and question marks come off, inverted Spanish punctuation comes off the front, a line like "cat, dog, joy" becomes three words, and a word your language's list already knows comes back spelled the way the list spells it. The aim is that you photograph a worksheet, the review screen opens already correct, and you tap Import without editing anything.

The rule underneath all of it: when the app is unsure, it flags and never deletes. Nothing with a letter in it disappears. A header like "Week 3 Spelling List" is left exactly as photographed, unticked, for you to judge — the old behaviour chopped it into three separate words and silently threw away the 3. A word with a digit in it, like 3D, used to vanish before you ever saw it; now it stays, unticked.

Numbering is only stripped when the page really is a numbered list — at least two lines and at least half of them. One stray "a." in a paragraph will not make the app start deleting the first letter of your words. And if the camera misreads "1." as "l.", that still counts.

Marks and accents are never removed. Neither is anything inside a word, so don't, well-being and l'eau survive intact, in every script.

What is most useful now:

• Photograph a real worksheet — ideally a numbered one — and tell me how many chips you had to edit before Import. Zero is the target.
• If something was deleted that should not have been, that is the most serious thing you can report. Tell me the line as it appears on the page.
• Lines with brackets, blanks to fill in, or words run together are the interesting cases.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
