The menu button is bigger, and the hub above the orb is down to four things: the wordmark, the menu button, the tagline, and the pill that says what you are playing. The Misses chip and the row of quick-play tiles are gone from that screen — everything they did is in the menu now, and the orb sits about 114 pixels higher because of it.

The menu button itself was, embarrassingly, never styled. It has been a bare 16-pixel-wide character since the menu shipped, with a tap target well under the size a button is supposed to have. It is now a proper 88 by 44 pill in the top-right corner, and it is the only thing up there.

Three things had quietly lost every way in, and two of those were my doing last build:

• Spell Off — the two-player, one-device game — was only ever reachable from its tile. It is in the menu now.
• Spell It was in the same position.
• "From a photo" has been unreachable since the last build. Its button was on the row I removed, and nothing replaced it. It is in Your Words in the menu.

There is now a test that walks every one of those and fails the build if any of them has no way in. It would have caught last build's mistake the day it happened.

Misses moved to the top of Your Words in the menu, with its count beside it — the same number as before, the words due for review right now, capped at 999+. When nothing is due the row is not there at all rather than sitting greyed out.

New: a small dot on the menu button when you have missed words you have not seen yet. It has no number, it never pulses, and it means NEW misses since you last opened the menu — not your whole pile, so a backlog does not leave it permanently lit. Opening the menu clears it; you do not have to go and review anything. Spell Jr never gets the dot at all.

What is most useful now:

• Everything you used to reach from the home screen should still be reachable from the menu. If something is not, that is the report I want most.
• Is the menu button obvious where it is, and easy to hit with your thumb?
• The whole screen below the orb has moved up. Does the keyboard land where your hands expect it?
• If you use Spell Off or Spell It, they should be in the menu under "Spell it".

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
