Game sounds and haptics, and everything from the last few builds in one place.

• Game sounds: soft taps and chimes. A right answer climbs a note with each answer in a row; a wrong one is a quiet low tone. They never play over the spoken word. Settings > Game sounds turns them off or sets their level, separate from the voice volume.
• Haptics: a tap for a right answer, a gentle double tap for a wrong one, and a light tick on each key. Settings > Haptics and Settings > Key feel turn them on or off.
• After a miss, the letters you typed are marked right in the answer box, with the correct spelling underneath, so the keyboard no longer gets pushed down.
• Back from earlier builds: the living orb, your Controls and Buttons colours, and the new keyboard. Impossible keeps every fix from build 294.
• Please play with sound on and tell me if any sound feels too loud, too quiet or out of place.
