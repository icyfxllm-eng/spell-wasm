Randospell: a dice game of spelling. Roll two dice, then spell a word with exactly that many letters.

Sprint: reach 50 points in as few rolls as you can. A 2 or a 12 pays double, and three right in a row adds a x1.5 bonus.
Marathon: right answers fill a pot. Bank it to keep the points, or roll again for more. A miss empties the pot, but banked points are safe, and one Ward saves a pot once.
Daily: the same dice and the same words for everyone today. One attempt.
Head-to-Head: pass the phone. A miss gives your rival a free bonus roll. First to 100 after a Bank wins.
Spell Jr: one die, a gentle second try after a miss, and no pressure.

Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Vietnamese, Arabic, Swahili and Filipino (Spell Jr: not Swahili or Filipino yet).

What is most useful now:

• Does the number on the die always match the letters in the word you hear?
• Marathon: does Bank or Roll again feel like a real decision?
• Daily: is it clear you only get one attempt?
• Head-to-Head: is the hand-off card clear, and does the bonus roll make sense?
• Spell Jr: is the second try encouraging, never scary?
• Languages: any word that is wrong, rude, too hard for its tier, or read in a strange voice.
• Small phone: any key, row or header that is clipped or too tight.
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.

To turn one mode off, tell me which; each can be switched off on its own.
