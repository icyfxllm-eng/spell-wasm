German, Russian and Korean have new voices. We put every candidate voice Google offers for those languages through two independent speech recognizers on the same sixty words, and the ones we were using came third, fourth and fifth. The new voices are heard correctly more often — five more words out of sixty in German, ten in Russian, fourteen in Korean. Every clip in all three languages has already been re-recorded, so nothing should be slow the first time you play it.

If you spell in German, Russian or Korean, the voice will sound different from yesterday. Whether it sounds BETTER to you is the thing we cannot measure, and it is the most useful thing you can tell us.

Spell Jr no longer serves the words dead, death, deaths, die, died, dies, kill, killed and killing. They stay in the game for adult play; they are filtered out when Spell Jr is on, the same way blood and grave already were. Six of the nine were in the easy and medium lists, which is exactly where Spell Jr plays.

The Impostor was badly broken and nobody had reported it. On the Easy setting it was dealing almost no rounds at all — across all fifteen languages it managed three rounds where the other settings manage a full set every time. Spell Jr always plays on Easy, so a child opening The Impostor got nothing. It was a rule about which wrong answers were allowed being impossible to satisfy for short words. Fixed, and there is now a test that plays every setting in every language and fails the build if any of them comes up short.

What is most useful now:

• German, Russian, Korean: does the new voice sound clearer, or just different? Either answer helps.
• If a specific word is hard to make out, tell us the word and the language.
• The Impostor on Easy should now deal a full round every time, in every language.
