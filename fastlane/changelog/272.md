This one is almost entirely Vietnamese. If you play in English, nothing visible changes — the mic work from 271 is unchanged and still the thing worth testing.

For Vietnamese, the app has been quietly wrong for a long time, and it took cleaning one thing to see it.

The Vietnamese word list was 73% not Vietnamese. English words, brand names, place names, and — my favourite — "retrieved", which is the word Wikipedia puts at the end of a citation. At the harder levels it was worse than four in five. Vietnamese players were mostly being asked to spell English.

The reason turned out not to be carelessness. A Vietnamese word is written as separate syllables with a space between them — học sinh, thành phố, cà phê — and the word list could only hold single words with no spaces. So 86% of real Vietnamese vocabulary could not be in the list at all, and a single Vietnamese syllable is too short to fill the harder levels. Something had to fill them, and English did.

The word list can now hold Vietnamese words, and the keyboard has a space key. You do not have to use it: an answer typed without the space is still correct, exactly as before. The list is now 5,680 real Vietnamese words, and the hard levels are words like đầu tiên and khoa học rather than fragments — or dreadnought.

The Daily puzzle is back for Vietnamese too. It had stopped being possible when the English padding was removed, which is its own uncomfortable fact: the Vietnamese Daily had been built out of English words.

One thing was taken away. Spelling out loud is switched off for Vietnamese. It could never work: Vietnamese tone marks are part of the spelling, and the app has no way to say one — you could spell the skeleton of a word and never the word itself. It was failing every time, so it is better not offered while that is true. Russian is unaffected, for the curious reason that Russian stress marks never form part of the answer you type.

What is most useful now:

• Vietnamese: does the space key behave? Can you spell a two-syllable word and have it accepted?
• Vietnamese: do the harder levels look like real words to you now? If anything there is still not a Vietnamese word, I want to know.
• Everyone else: 271's spelling-out-loud work is still the thing to push on, especially spelling at your natural speed rather than slowing down for it.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
