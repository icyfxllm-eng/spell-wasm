This build is the one to test with your voice. Please spell a word out loud and tell me what happens.

The microphone has not been working. It turned on, the orange recording dot appeared, and the app received nothing at all — not quiet audio, literally no sound data — so it waited, decided it had heard no speech, and gave up. Spelling a word perfectly made no difference, because nothing you said ever arrived.

The cause was the phone's audio system, not the listening. A phone has one audio session, and it is either set up to play sound or to record it. Playing a word put it in play mode, which has no microphone attached. If a word played while the mic was listening, the microphone was quietly disconnected for the rest of that session. The app now has a single place that decides this, and while the mic is listening a request to play audio waits its turn instead of cutting the mic off.

Two smaller things came with it. The mic can now always be switched off by tapping it again — on some languages a second tap did nothing at all, because the "stop" was behind checks that returned first. And after using the mic, word audio goes back to the volume and silent-switch behaviour it had before, rather than staying in recording mode.

What is most useful now, roughly in order:

• Tap the orb to hear a word, tap the mic, and say the letters: "C, A, T". Do the letters appear? This is the whole build.
• Tap the mic and say nothing. It should give up after about six seconds and the orange dot should go out within a second.
• Tap the mic, then tap it again. It should stop, every time. Try it ten times.
• After using the mic, tap Replay. The word should be as loud as it was before, and with the silent switch on, word audio should still be audible.
• Try it in airplane mode. Recognition is on your device, so it should behave identically.
• If you have the developer diagnostic line on, a screenshot of it after speaking is worth more than any description — it now shows the audio route, the sample rate, the format, and how many chunks of sound arrived.

If the letters still do not appear, that screenshot is the single most useful thing you can send.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
