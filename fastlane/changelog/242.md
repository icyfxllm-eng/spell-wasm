"Can't hear it" is now in every mode, and two screens that used to run off the bottom of a phone now fit on one.

Tap "Can't hear it" in SpellDoku, Spell Search or Spell Cross and you can hear the word again, slowly. None of those three had a slow replay before — Spell Search only gave you one on the easier levels, and the other two never did. In the main game it also still offers to show you the word, which costs you nothing: that round simply does not count, your streak stays, and a replacement word takes its place.

SpellDoku does not offer to show you the word, on purpose. A letter there stands for a word, and telling you one would solve every cell with that letter for the rest of the board. Hint is still there for being properly stuck.

Spell Cross and Spell Search fit a phone now. Spell Cross was the bad one — on a small phone the grid, the clues and the keyboard together ran about 450 points past the bottom of the screen. The crossword grid now sizes itself to the space it has, the clue list scrolls in its own box, and backspace sits on the end of the bottom letter row. Spell Search got the same for its word list. Worth a look on the hardest levels, where the squares get small: if a square is too small to tap comfortably, say which level and which phone.

Audio: every word now has a little silence added before and after it, so the first and last sounds cannot be clipped as playback starts. Clips are still being regenerated, so the first time you play a particular word it may take a moment longer.

If a word is still hard to make out after hearing it slowly, that is the thing we most want to know — tell us the word and the language.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
