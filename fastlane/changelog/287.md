A visual update: the first step of a cleaner, calmer look.

• The play screen now fits every iPhone without scrolling, including the smaller SE models. Check spelling sits next to Delete on the keyboard, and "It was ..." appears right under your answer.
• The menu (☰) has a proper look instead of plain grey buttons.
• Arabic, Hindi and Vietnamese words use their own fonts throughout, and the English around them no longer switches to an old-style typeface. Chinese, Japanese and Korean text uses the iPhone's own regional fonts.
• With Reduce Motion on (Settings > Accessibility > Motion), nothing slides, shakes or bounces any more; colours still show right and wrong.
• Everything from build 286 (Impossible) is still here.
• Please play a few rounds on your phone and tell me about anything that looks cut off, overlapping or hard to reach.
