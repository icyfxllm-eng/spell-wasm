This build changes the English definitions and example sentences. Everything else is the same as build 316.

What changed:
• Every definition and example sentence is new, written for children and checked by hand. The Definition and Sentence buttons, the result card and Definition Match all use them.
• A few words that don't belong in a children's spelling game are gone.
• If a word has no checked definition yet, it simply shows none.

What is most useful now:
• Play a few rounds and tap Definition and Sentence. Do they make sense for the word, and is the sentence read aloud clearly?
• Play Definition Match at a couple of levels, with and without Kid Mode. Does each round deal cleanly, with no repeats and no stuck screen?
• Any definition or sentence that seems wrong, confusing or not right for a child. Tell us the word.
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
