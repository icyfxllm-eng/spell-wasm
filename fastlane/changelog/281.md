Randospell grows up: the whole v1.1 table, switched on for this device pass.

Heat: each right word you do not bank adds a pip, and the pips multiply your next word (up to ×3). A bank or a miss puts the heat out.
Guess the roll: before you press, pick Low, Seven or High. A right guess and a right word add a bonus.
Stretch: after the die lands you can take a harder word for ×1.5 (only where a harder word exists).
No replay: hear the word once for ×1.5.
Doubles: when the orb glows gold the two dice matched and the word pays double.
Table: play one to three computer players: Cautious, Daring or Mirror, at Easy, Normal or Tough.
Steal: on a 7 at the table you can try to take half of a rival's unbanked orbit with a hard word. Not in solo play.
Final Round: when someone banks to 100 everyone else gets one last turn.
Daily: the same dice and words for everyone today, with the guesses and charms.
Head-to-Head and Spell Jr (one die, a second try, a racing computer player) are on too.
Eclipse visuals: a dial that stops on your roll, stones orbiting a moon for your orbit, heat notches and a corona. Reduce Motion turns the movement off and leaves the numbers.

What is most useful now:

• Does Stretch ever show up in English? (It is rare there by design.)
• Table: do the computer players feel beatable on Normal? Does Mirror make sense?
• Steal: did you ever get to try one? It needs a rival to hold an orbit when your turn comes.
• Final Round: is it clear everyone gets one last turn?
• Eclipse: does the orbit read at a glance, and does anything slow the phone down?
• Spell Jr: is the second try and the racer friendly?
• Spanish: any word or line that reads wrong.
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
