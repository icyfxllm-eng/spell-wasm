This build is the English edition. Spell now plays English only, so the other languages are gone from the app for now. Your saved word lists in other languages are kept, but they can't be played in this build.

What changed:
• Nothing you type or say leaves your phone. Answers are checked on the device, and Spell It recognizes your speech on the device. There are no accounts and no leaderboard sign-in.
• English Easy, the Daily Challenge and Spell Jr are free. SpellGame English unlocks every other level. Purchases in TestFlight use Apple's sandbox, so buying is free. Please buy; the paywall is the thing to test.
• British spellings are accepted: colour, centre, organise.

What is most useful now:
• Play with Airplane Mode on after the first few words. Does grading still work? Which words go silent?
• Buy SpellGame English, then delete the app, reinstall from TestFlight and check the purchase comes back.
