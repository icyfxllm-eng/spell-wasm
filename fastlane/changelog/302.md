The keyboard comes alive, for your Phase C review.

• Letters fly: each letter you type rises from its key into your answer. Type fast and they skip the flight.
• Constellation: after a right answer, the keys you used light up as stars joined in the order you typed, then fade. Tap to skip it.
• The keyboard reacts: a wave across the keys when you're right, a soft amber dim when you're not, and the keys warm up as your streak grows.
• Modes that keep their verdict to themselves (a solo Spelluzzle board, SpellDoku) only get a neutral pulse, so the keys never give anything away.
• With Reduce Motion on, nothing flies or waves; only colours change.
• New in Settings: Show accents as keys, for anyone who can't long-press.

Please play a few words in a couple of modes and tell me if any of it feels too much, too little, or gets in the way.
