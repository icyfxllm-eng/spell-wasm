A small one, on top of everything in 242.

The English word list no longer asks you to spell British-spelled words in an American voice — "theatre", "travelled" and a few others are out, since the voice you hear is American. Either spelling was always accepted and still is; the game just stops asking for the one that does not match the voice. Portuguese had the same tidy-up: two European words came out of a Brazilian-voiced list.

Everything from 242 is still the thing worth testing, if you have not yet:

• "Can't hear it" on every mode. In SpellDoku, Spell Search and Spell Cross it plays the word again slowly — none of those had that before. In the main game it will also show you the word, and that round then does not count.
• Spell Cross and Spell Search on a phone. Both used to run off the bottom of the screen; Spell Cross badly. If a square is too small to tap on the harder levels, tell us the level and the phone.
• Audio. Words now carry a little silence at each end so the first and last sounds are not clipped. Clips are regenerated the first time a word is played, so an occasional word may take a moment longer — that settles down.

If a word is still hard to make out after hearing it slowly, that is the most useful thing you can report: the word and the language.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
