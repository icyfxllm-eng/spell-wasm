SpellDoku changed in three ways worth trying.

The rules are enforced. Putting the same letter or number twice in a row, column or box is now refused outright, on every difficulty. Before this build a Hard board would quietly accept three of the same letter in one box. If you can still get a duplicate onto a board, that is the bug we most want to hear about.

You pick the symbol first, then spell it. Tap a cell, tap the letter or number you want, and then spell its word. That is why a conflict can be refused before you have spelled anything. Each symbol now shows how many of it are left, and on Easy and Medium the ones that cannot go in the cell you picked are dimmed.

You choose Numbers or Letters, per difficulty. The chip at the top of the board is now a picker: Numbers, Letters, or Mix, which alternates. Easy and Medium start on Numbers, Hard and Expert on Letters, and your choice sticks per difficulty. Changing it never disturbs the board you are on — it applies to the next one. Your first Letters board explains itself, and "?" at the top brings that back.

Two more things: 12x12 is gone, and the Daily is now different for every player and different every time you play it, in SpellDoku, Spell Cross and Spell Search alike.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
