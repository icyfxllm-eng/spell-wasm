The microphone was turned all the way down. This build turns it up.

Voice spelling has been failing for a specific and slightly embarrassing reason. The app was asking the phone for a recording mode called "measurement" — a setting whose entire purpose is to switch OFF the automatic processing and gain that normally makes a voice usable. It is the right choice for an app measuring sound levels scientifically. It is the wrong choice for listening to someone spell a word. Paired with another setting that forces audio to the speaker, the incoming sound arrived about a hundred times too quiet to recognise. Every letter spoken was technically captured, dutifully passed along, and then correctly judged to contain no speech, because at that volume it did not.

The recording mode is now the ordinary one, the speaker setting is gone, and where the phone allows it the input level is set explicitly.

Two related corrections. The app decides "that was speech" by comparing against a loudness threshold; it now measures the actual quiet of your room first, and does so within a fraction of a second rather than half of one. And when sound does arrive carrying no signal at all, the app now says "The microphone isn't sending sound" instead of "Didn't catch that — try again". The second blames you for a fault in the app, and that wrong message is a large part of why this took as long as it did to find.

What is most useful now:

• Tap the orb, tap the mic, say "C, A, T" slowly. Do the letters land in the box?
• Watch the small readout underneath while you speak. The number after "rms=" should jump clearly when you talk. If it stays near zero while you are speaking into the phone, that is the answer I need, with a photo.
• "spoke=y" means your voice registered. "spoke=n" while you are clearly talking means this is still not fixed.
• Word audio, Replay, Slow and typing should all be exactly as before. Anything different there is a bug.

I will not promise this is the end of it. Every previous build ruled something out and this one has the best evidence behind it yet, but the proof is letters appearing on your screen, not anything I can check from here.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
