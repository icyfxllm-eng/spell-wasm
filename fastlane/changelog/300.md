Persian (فارسی) is back, for its native-speaker audit.

• Pick فارسی in the language menu: right-to-left, the Persian keyboard with all 32 letters on the main layer, about 8,300 words across the four levels, spoken by an Azure Persian voice.
• Your spelling is what counts, not your keyboard: an Arabic-style ي or ك from a phone keyboard, a missing half-space (می‌شود typed as میشود) and ا for آ are all marked correct. A different letter, like س for ص, is still wrong.
• Not in Persian yet, shown as "coming soon": Practice, Definition Match, Impossible and spelling out loud.
• Everything from build 299 is still here.
• Persian speakers: please flag any word that is a name, misspelled, not real Persian or not right for children, and any word the voice says wrong.
