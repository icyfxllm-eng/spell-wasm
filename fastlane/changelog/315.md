This build brings the Eclipse look back: the custom symbols, the day and night themes, the sounds and haptics, and the keyboard that reacts as you type. Everything else is the English edition from build 314.

What changed:
• The look from earlier builds is back. Build 314 showed plain emoji and one theme by mistake.
• The purchase sheet now offers only SpellGame English, plus Restore Purchases.
• Nothing you type or say leaves your phone, and English Easy, the Daily Challenge and Spell Jr stay free.

What is most useful now:
• Do the symbols, themes and celebrations look right? Switch between day and night.
• Open a locked level. Does the sheet show one purchase, SpellGame English, with a real price?
• Buy it, delete the app, reinstall from TestFlight and Restore. Is everything back?
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
