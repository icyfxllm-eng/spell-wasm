Another voice-spelling build, and this one found something specific.

The app was listening to you. The microphone was working, the sound was arriving, and it was being handed to the part that recognises speech — all of that was fine. The problem was a threshold. The app decides "that was speech, not room noise" by comparing loudness against a fixed number, and that number was set five times higher than what the microphone on a real phone actually delivers. So every letter you said was filed as silence, and nothing ever landed.

That number is no longer fixed. Each time you tap the mic, the app measures how quiet the room actually is for a fraction of a second, and treats anything meaningfully above that as speech. A fixed figure cannot be right for every phone and every room; the one measured in that moment can be.

Two other things changed. The diagnostic readout used to share a line with "Listening… say the letters", so the message erased it the instant you started — exactly when it mattered. It has its own line now, and updates several times a second while you speak, so you can watch the level move. And the white box some of you saw when scrolling, especially with the keyboard open, is gone — the background simply did not extend that far.

What is most useful now:

• Tap the orb, tap the mic, say "C, A, T" slowly. Do the letters land? That is the whole question.
• Watch the small readout while you speak. The number after "rms=" should jump when you talk and fall when you stop. If it does not move at all, that is the single most useful thing you can tell me, with a photo.
• "spoke=y" in that line means the app registered your voice as speech. "spoke=n" while you are clearly talking is the bug, still alive.
• Spelling with the keyboard is unchanged, as always. If anything about typing, word audio, Replay or Slow is different, that is a bug.

I would rather tell you this is probably fixed than certainly fixed. The level on a real phone is very low, and if it turns out the microphone is simply not being given enough gain, there is one more change after this one.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
