The new Spell keyboard, for your device pass.

Every keyboard in the app now runs on one shared Spell keyboard: the main game, Spell Picture, Bee, Word Chains, Board Game, Randospell, Spelluzzle, SpellDoku, Spell Search and Spell Cross. Nothing about grading changed.

Please type a few words in each language and tell me anything that feels wrong:
• Keys show lowercase, and go uppercase only while Shift is on (German Impossible).
• Long-press a letter for its accents, now in Bee and Word Chains too.
• Vietnamese: the tone row works in Spell Picture, Bee and Word Chains, and the five tone keys have their own names for VoiceOver.
• Board Game in German can now give an Impossible trap word, with a Shift key to type its capitals.
• Settings > Use device keyboard: answer with your phone's own keyboard instead (main play screen). Autocorrect stays off.

Everything from build 289 is still here: the brighter controls, the living orb and amber feedback.
