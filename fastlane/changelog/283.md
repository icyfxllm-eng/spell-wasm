Drawn Quick Bee, for your handwriting pass (Chinese and Japanese). Randospell is as in build 282.

Open it from the Play list as "Quick Bee · Draw" with your language set to Chinese or Japanese (Korean is held back until it has been measured, and Spell Jr does not get it).

How it plays: tap Start, listen to the word, and draw it one character at a time. You have 60 seconds and there is no confirm button: when your pen rests, the character is read. Every right character scores a point. A wrong character ends that word (what you had so far keeps its points) and the next word starts. If a character could not be read at all you get one free redraw per word.

What I most need from you:

• Does it read your handwriting? Try about 50 characters per language; note the ones it refuses.
• The pause: is the wait after you lift the pen about right, or does it cut you off / feel slow?
• Does the pad feel the right size on your phone?
• Chinese: is the voice reading the right word every time?
• Japanese: is it fair at Easy and Medium (it is all kana)?
• Anything that crashed, froze, or showed text in the wrong place.
