Two things here: an apology, and the fix I think finally matters.

First the apology. The previous build made things worse, and it was my fault in a specific and avoidable way. To help diagnose this, I added one extra piece of information to the live readout — whether the speech recogniser reports itself available. Reading that value happens to require waiting on another part of the system, and I put it in the one place where waiting is forbidden: the code that runs each time a fresh slice of microphone audio arrives. That code must finish instantly. Mine paused, and the audio stopped flowing after a few slices. The diagnostic destroyed the thing it was added to observe. It is gone from there now, with a note explaining why nobody should put it back.

That matters because the build before it was actually working: your voice was arriving loud and clear. That progress was real and it still stands.

Now the fix. With the microphone working, the remaining problem was that the speech recogniser returned nothing. The reason is that the app was interrupting it. It listened for a pause, and when it heard about a third of a second of quiet it closed off that piece of recognition and started a new one. Spelling out loud — "W… I… R… E" — there is a pause after every letter, so the recogniser was being shut down after each one, long before it had enough to work with. It reported hearing no speech, which was honest from where it sat.

The recogniser now listens continuously for as long as the microphone is on, and letters appear as it works them out. This is how the design always said it should work.

What is most useful now:

• Tap the orb, tap the mic, spell the word out loud one letter at a time with normal pauses. Do the letters appear?
• If they appear but come out wrong or jumbled, that is a different and much better problem — tell me what you said and what you got.
• If nothing appears, the readout now shows "err=" with a specific code when the recogniser complains, and that code is what I need.
• Typing, word audio, Replay and Slow are untouched.

New here? Tap the glowing orb to hear a word, then spell it on the keyboard and press Check. Everything works offline after the first load.
