Spell is an English spelling game. This release is English only. There is no account or sign-in anywhere in the app.

How to test:
1. Tap the glowing orb to hear a word.
2. Type the spelling and tap "Check spelling." Grading happens on the device.
3. The Easy level, the Daily Challenge and Spell Jr are free.
4. Choose Medium or higher in the difficulty menu to see the purchase sheet. An adult gate appears first: "Ask a grown-up", with a question such as "What is four times seven?" The purchase is "SpellGame English". Restore Purchases is on the sheet.
5. Settings > Purchases opens the same sheet at any time, including Restore Purchases.

Spell Jr never shows a purchase sheet.

Data: no personal data is collected. Answers are graded on the device. The app requests word audio and definitions from our server; those requests contain only the word, with no identifier. It also fetches a signed list of feature switches, which can only turn features off. Progress is stored only on the device.

Microphone: used only by the optional "Spell It" tool (Settings > Tools & Features, off by default). Speech is recognized on the device and never sent anywhere.
