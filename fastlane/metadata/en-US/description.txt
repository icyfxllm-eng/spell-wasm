Spell is a spelling game you play with your ears. Hear a word, then spell it. Keep your chain going and climb from easy words to expert ones.

WHY SPELL
• Learn by listening. Every word is spoken clearly, so you practice going from sound to spelling, the skill real spelling tests measure.
• Climb the chain. Words get harder as your streak grows. Miss one and it comes back later on a spaced-repetition schedule.
• Understand words. After each answer you see the word's meaning and an example sentence.
• British or American. Colour or color, organise or organize: both are right.

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Spell Jr for younger players, free at every level.
• Big Text for younger or low-vision players.
• Spell Off: pass one device and see who builds the longest chain.
