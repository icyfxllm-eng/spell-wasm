Spell — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Spell is a spelling game published by the maker of spellgame.net. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when you use the Spell app or the website.

The short version: Spell collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your answers are checked on your device, and when you spell out loud your voice is recognized on your device. Your progress stays on your device. To play a word, the app asks our server for that word's sound and meaning; that request contains the word and nothing about you.

Playing the game

You play Spell without an account. Your streaks, statistics, settings, word lists and choices are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and they are removed if you delete the app or clear its data. Your answers are checked on your device; they are not sent anywhere.

What the app asks our server for

To play a word, the app asks our server for that word's pronunciation, its definition and an example sentence. The request contains the word and nothing else: no name, account, device ID or advertising ID. Our server makes and caches each word's sound with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and may look the word up in Wiktionary or a public dictionary. Those services see our server, not you, and the cache is kept by word, not by person.

The app also downloads one small signed file from our server that can only switch features off. The request carries no information.

All traffic uses HTTPS and reaches our server through Cloudflare, our network provider, which sees the IP address of each connection, as any network provider does. Our server does not keep a log of your requests or your IP address.

Purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store with your Apple Account. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app learns only which of our products you own, so it can unlock them.

Crash reports

Spell has no crash or analytics reporting of its own. If you have chosen in your iPhone or iPad settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us crash reports. Apple controls that setting and what those reports contain.

Microphone and camera

Microphone. The optional "Spell It" tool lets you say the letters instead of typing. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, and your speech is recognized by your device's own speech recognition. The audio never leaves your device.

Camera and photos. Where available, you can photograph a spelling list, or pick a photo of one, to turn it into your own word list. The photo is read on your device and is not uploaded. This feature is not available in Spell Jr.

Language votes (website only)

The Spell app plays English and has no language vote. On spellgame.net, choosing a language the site does not play shows a "Vote for this language" button. Tapping it sends our server the language code and a random ID that the site creates in your browser the first time you tap. The ID is not your name, email, device name or advertising ID. We use it only so that repeated taps from the same browser count once. We keep these IDs, grouped by language, for as long as we are counting interest; because the ID is random, we can't look up which one is yours. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the copy there.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, it is scheduled on your device. We do not run a push notification server and cannot send you messages.

Children's privacy

Spell collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Spell Jr shows no purchases and no camera, and nothing a child types or says leaves the device. Every purchase sits behind a question for grown-ups, and Apple's Ask to Buy applies to purchases made on a child's Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Spell handles information, we will update this page and revise the date above before the change takes effect.
