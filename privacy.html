Spell — Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

Spell is a spelling game published by the maker of spellgame.net. We built Spell to be private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what happens to information when you use the app or the website.

The short version: Playing Spell requires no account and collects no personal information — your progress stays on your device. The only time we collect personal data is if you choose to create an account to compete on the online leaderboard ("The Climb"). We never show ads, and we use no third-party analytics or tracking. A few features do need our server to work — checking English answers, spelling out loud on some devices, and (on the website) voting for a language — and each is explained below, including exactly what is sent and what is kept. If you leave "Help improve SpellGame" on, the app also sends us anonymous crash and speed reports. They never include your words, your answers, or anything about you, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Playing the game — no data collected

You can play Spell fully without an account. Your gameplay — streaks, statistics, settings, custom word lists, and language choices — is saved only in your device's local storage. It stays on your device, is never sent to us except as described below, and is removed if you delete the app or clear its data.

What the app sends to our server to play

To play a word, the app asks our server (spellgame.net) for that word's pronunciation audio and dictionary definition. For this, only the dictionary word is sent — never anything about you. Our server generates and caches audio for common words (using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, and Microsoft Azure Speech for Swahili) so the app loads quickly; this cache is by word, not by user. All traffic uses HTTPS and reaches our server through Cloudflare, our network provider. We keep standard, short-lived server logs for security and reliability; they are not used to identify or profile you.

Checking English answers. When you answer an English word, the app sends the word and the answer you typed to our server, which replies only "correct" or "not correct". Your answer is compared and then discarded: our server does not save it or write it to its logs, and it is not linked to you or your device. This happens in every mode, including Spell Jr and the Education edition. If our server can't be reached, the app checks your answer on your device instead.

Crash and speed reports ("Help improve SpellGame")

To find problems before players do, the app can send us short, anonymous reports when something breaks or runs slowly. This is on by default for players 13 and over, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Help improve SpellGame. Turning it off also deletes any reports still waiting on your device.

What a report contains:

  • The type of problem (for example "the app crashed" or "a sound couldn't play"), and a code for where in our program it happened.
  • The app version, whether you're on iPhone, Android or the web, the language you were studying, and which game mode you were last in.
  • How long some things took, in broad ranges (for example "under a quarter of a second"), such as starting the app or playing a word's sound.
  • For players 13 and over, a random number that changes every time the app starts, so reports from one session can be grouped. It's never saved on your device and can't be used to recognize you later.

What a report never contains: the words you practice, what you type, your answers or scores, your recordings or photos, your word lists, your account, email or username, or your device's advertising ID. We don't record your IP address when a report arrives: our reporting service never reads it, and no report is stored with it. (Cloudflare, our network provider, handles every connection to spellgame.net and keeps a sample of network traffic, including IP addresses, for its security tools. That sample is never connected to reports, and we don't use it for them.) Reports are stored separately from Climb accounts, with no way to connect the two.

Spell Jr and younger players. On a device in Spell Jr, or before the birthday question has been answered, the app never sends individual reports. It keeps a simple tally of how many times each kind of problem happened and sends it at most once a day, at a random time, with no identifier of any kind. A grown-up can turn this off through the parent check in Settings.

Schools. In the Education edition, reports are off until the school turns them on, and even then only the daily tally is sent.

How long we keep them. Individual reports are deleted after 90 days. We keep daily totals (for example "12 crashes on version X in Russian"), which contain nothing about any person.

Who handles them. Reports go to our own service, which runs on Cloudflare. No analytics or advertising company receives them.

Language votes (website only)

The Spell app plays English and has no language vote. On spellgame.net, choosing a language the site does not play shows a "Vote for this language" button. Tapping it sends our server the language code and a random ID that the site creates in your browser the first time you tap. The ID is not your name, email, device name or advertising ID, and it is not linked to a Climb account. We use it only so that repeated taps from the same browser count once when we count interest in each language. If you are offline, the tap is saved and sent the next time the site opens.

We keep these IDs, grouped by language, for as long as we are counting interest. Because the ID is random and not connected to you, we can't look up which one is yours. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the copy there. Voting does not sign you up for any email or message; see Notifications below.

The Climb — optional accounts & leaderboard

"The Climb" is an optional online leaderboard. It is the only part of Spell that collects personal information, and only if you create an account. If you never create one, none of the following applies to you.

When you create a Climb account, we store:

  • Username and display name — shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Email address — used to verify your account and to reset your password. Not shown publicly.
  • Phone number — only if you choose to add one for SMS password reset. Never shown publicly.
  • Password — stored only as a salted bcrypt hash. We never store or log your actual password.
  • Your leaderboard results — your best word chain per difficulty, when you achieved it, and basic run details (e.g. word count and duration) used to rank fairly and detect cheating.

We use this information only to run the leaderboard: to sign you in, show and rank scores, verify your email, reset your password, prevent abuse, and let players report inappropriate usernames. We do not sell or share it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Service providers. To send verification and password-reset messages we use an email provider, and to block automated sign-ups we use Cloudflare Turnstile (an anti-bot check that receives your IP address to score the request — we do not store your IP address). These providers process the minimum needed to perform their function.

Delete your account any time. The app has a "Delete account" option that permanently removes your account and all associated data — profile, leaderboard entries, and sessions — from our database. You can also email us to request deletion.

Microphone and camera

The optional handwriting feature recognizes letters you draw on your own screen; the drawing is processed on your device and is not uploaded.

Microphone. On iPhone and iPad you can speak instead of typing. Normally your device's own speech recognition handles this: the audio is recognized on your device and never sent to us.

Some languages can't be recognized on some devices. For those, you can choose to use our server instead. The app asks first, on a one-time screen, and only uses the server if you tap "Use the internet". After that, while the microphone is on, the app sends short audio clips of what you say to our server. Each clip goes with the language and a fixed list of that alphabet's letter names, which helps recognition. Our server passes the clip to Google Cloud Speech-to-Text, which acts as our service provider and returns the recognized text. Our server does not save the audio or write it to its logs, and it is not linked to you or your device. This option is never offered in Spell Jr or in the Education edition.

Camera and photos. On iPhone and iPad you can photograph a spelling list, or pick a photo of one, to turn it into your own word list. The photo is read on your device and is not uploaded. This feature is not available in Spell Jr.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily practice reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a push-notification server and cannot send you messages. Voting for a language on the website records your interest (see above) but does not sign you up for any notification.

Children's privacy

The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Creating a Climb account requires an email address and is intended for players old enough to have one; children playing in Spell Jr do not create accounts, and no personal information is collected from them. In Spell Jr the app never sends voice audio to our server and the camera feature is hidden. Typed English answers are still checked by our server, as described above, and are not stored. Crash and speed reports from Spell Jr are only an anonymous daily tally (see "Crash and speed reports" above). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
