## R5. Definition Match draw (N)

- `POOL_FLOOR = 40` (src/defmatch.rs:83); `CARDS_KID = 3`, `CARDS_FULL = 4` (:86-87); every 4th round is reversed (`REVERSE_EVERY = 4`).
- **Run length:** a run is open-ended and ends when the player taps Close. A `USED` set (defmatch_screen.rs:92, cleared on open :215) keeps targets from repeating for |eligible| rounds, then resets.
- **Distractors:** drawn from the whole eligible pool. They never repeat within a round, but may repeat across rounds. A round needs at least n_cards − 1 candidates.
- **N = 40 reviewed rows per tier** (Kid: 40 that are also kid-register). That is the existing floor, and it gives 40 rounds with no repeated target.
- **Below the floor today:**
  - The only hide is per language (`consts::def_match`); there is no per-tier hide.
  - On open, the screen steps down from expert to easy until `ensure_pool` passes. But `ensure_pool`'s floor (defmatch_screen.rs:302) counts only `prompt_grade` across all rows, ignoring kid-register. So a Kid view can pass it while `generate` returns None. `next_round` then shows `so.errGeneric` with no rounds (:352-354). That is a stuck screen, a bug D-DEF6 / A15.5 will cover.
- `pool_floor_met` (defmatch.rs:200) has no caller outside defmatch.rs.
