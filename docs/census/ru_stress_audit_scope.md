# Russian stress audit — what the pass actually buys

Prepared 2026-10-06. The sheet is `build/ru-stress-sheet.csv`; the key it is
graded against is `build/ru-stress-sheet-DECOY-KEY.csv`.

`config/ru-stress-audit.json` has said `audited: false` since 2026-08-29, with
the note that Eric flips it after his own pass, expecting about a month of study
first. Before spending that month, three measurements.

## 1. All 973 rows are real judgements

No filler. The ingest already excluded the two row classes that need no human:

* single-vowel words — stress has only one place it can go;
* words containing ё — ё is always stressed in Russian and already written.

973 rows, 973 decisions. Nothing can be skimmed.

## 2. Hard and expert are unmarked BY DESIGN — correcting this document

An earlier version of this file read the table's coverage (easy 67%, medium 81%,
hard 0%, expert 0%) as a gap and recommended extending the table before the
audit. That was wrong, and `tools/ru_stress_annotate.py` says so plainly:

> v2 Feature 4: "Marked stress applies to easy and normal only. Hard and expert
> use natural unmarked speech: decoding reduced vowels unaided is the advanced
> listening skill." So the audit is 1135 words, not 5961 — hard and expert keep
> all 4973 of their entries and play exactly as they do now, they simply never
> carry a mark.

The zero is the design. Hard and expert players are supposed to decode reduced
vowels without help, because that is the skill being taught. Extending the table
there would have removed an intended difficulty, and no test would have caught
it — `AUDITED_TIERS` is a constant in the annotator, not an invariant.

So the audit is one pass, not two, and it is 1,135 rows rather than 5,961.

## 3. A heteronym cannot be stored

`RU_STRESS` is `(&str, u8)` — one index per spelling, and 973 distinct words
with no duplicates. A word whose stress changes its meaning therefore cannot be
represented: за́мок (castle) and замо́к (lock) are one row, and whichever is
stored makes the other silently wrong.

Not live today: none of the ten classic heteronyms checked (замок, мука, атлас,
орган, хлопок, духи, белки, кружки, стрелки, пропасть) is in the table. But the
annotator already anticipates them — it flags "source gives more than one stress
for this spelling" — so the sheet can RAISE a heteronym that the table cannot
then STORE. Worth settling before a refill adds one.

## The sheet is ready, and the annotator made a better one

`build/ru-stress-sheet-full.csv` — **1,135 rows**, generated by
`tools/ru_stress_annotate.py` from `tools/lexicon-ingest/sources/kaikki-ru.jsonl`
(893MB, already present, gitignored as a build input like the Leipzig corpora).

    exact surface match, pre-filled to VERIFY   973
    source gives more than one stress  (BLANK)  112
    stress moves under inflection      (BLANK)   30
    no match at all                    (BLANK)   20
                      the auditor fills          162

A guess is never written. An exact surface-form match is a value; a lemma-only
match is a flag with an empty index, because Russian stress moves under
inflection — рука́ becomes ру́ку, нача́ть becomes на́чал — and reusing a lemma's
index is wrong across large swathes of the bank AND FAILS SILENTLY, since the
spelling still looks right and only a listener notices.

The 112 blanks are the heteronym problem arriving in practice: the source itself
gives two stresses for вода, волна, деньги and 109 others, and §3 below explains
why the table can only store one. These are the rows where an auditor is doing
something a machine provably cannot.

Six decoys are planted, scattered and unlabelled, weighted toward inflected forms
whose lemma stress differs — the exact defect the spec calls the
highest-probability one in the workstream. More than one miss rejects the sheet.
The key is `build/ru-stress-sheet-full-DECOY-KEY.csv` and must not go to the
auditor.

    python3 tools/ru_stress_ingest.py \
      --sheet build/ru-stress-sheet-full.csv \
      --key   build/ru-stress-sheet-full-DECOY-KEY.csv --write

then flip `audited` in `config/ru-stress-audit.json` by hand, because no tool
sets a human claim.

## One observation about who does it

The decoy mechanism exists to gate PAYMENT (v2 Feature 2). The workflow was
built expecting a hired native auditor, not a month of self-study — the sheet,
the decoys and the tolerance are all machinery for trusting someone else's work.
Handing this sheet to a Russian speaker is the path it was designed for. Eric's
call, but the month is not the only option.
